## Divergence log

Each entry: what the PRD says → what this session clarified → the words that carry it.

### 1. S is the conductor — the orchestration *is* S

- **PRD:** R2 has "S in every phase"; §5.3 seats S as "centre, midwife". But the PRD treats the
  conductor (D1, dsh-hosted) as a *separate* component — a layer that walks the desks.
- **Clarified:** the conductor is not a layer above the desks. **The conductor is S.** S is the centre
  of *every* cell, at *every* depth, so the orchestration is the recurring act of starting from
  not-knowing — not a fifth thing bolted on.
- **Amihai's words:** *"S is the membrane. S is the gate where the infinite zero is allowed by a human
  start from not knowing... it's only natural that S will be the orchestrator, the conductor, call it
  whatever you want. Because what we say, start from not knowing, is the ultimate universal creation
  potential. And human and AI steer that together. That's the constitution."*
- **Consequence for the build:** S may be **an empty file** — a bare signless start, no AI of its own.
  The conduction activates an AI *at* S to do the delicate dance: converse, encourage start-from-
  not-knowing by making it hard to cheat, and articulate the question that matters. S governs
  direction — passive in that sense — while carrying the wisdom of the dance. D1 (where the conductor
  is *hosted*) is unchanged; it is no longer read as "a component separate from S."

### 2. Every phase contains all five — the Holographic Law made operational

- **PRD:** R1 "cell is the only shape" + R2 "S in every phase" already state the law. D5 = "one cell +
  descent".
- **Clarified:** the operational consequence, which the PRD leaves implicit. The desk structure is
  **one grammar seated at addresses** over `{S, G, Q, P, V}`, **never five flat desk files**. Click S →
  the full cell. Click Q → Q's full cell (S·within·Q, G·within·Q, …). The centre of every cell is S —
  the question within that phase.
- **Amihai's words:** *"Every phase has the five phases in it. If you don't understand that, you're
  ignoring the Fractal, and what we're building is the Fractal... if I click on S, I need to see the
  cell, the full cell of the five windows."*
- **Consequence:** "scale by repeating the lawful cell, never by replacing the syntax." The sign (+/−)
  is navigation only, never inside an equation; zoom in = append a letter, zoom out = strip.

### 3. The initiation register — first person, not assignment

- **PRD:** does not specify the activation register of a desk's instruction.
- **Clarified:** a desk is activated by **self-speaking** ("I am…"), never by **assignment** ("you
  are…"). Assignment hands the agent a costume (K) — it goes hollow. Self-speaking is constitution.
- **Carried by:** the held activation grammar (`5qln-agent-activation`), whose per-phase passages are
  the five desks already written in the correct register. The "methodology" summary (third person) is
  for human eyes; the *initiation* (first person) is for the agent.
- **Consequence:** each desk's bundle opens with the codex seal + a first-person seat, not a job
  description.

### 4. The negative boundary is load-bearing

- **PRD:** §5.3 lists each desk's instruction but not its *negative* boundary as the structural joint.
- **Clarified:** each desk's "I will not…" line is what keeps the 4+1 from collapsing into one desk.
  S: "I will not originate the question." G: "I will not answer it." Q: "I will not force the
  intersection." P: "I will not plan the path." V: "I will not close without ∞0′."
- **Consequence:** the boundaries are first-class bundle content, not prose decoration.
